# Decision Outcomes

## Overview

Open a decision's **Settings** to read its configured outcomes. A compact
**Outcomes** block below **Winners** explains what winners receive or what
happens after approval. The block is omitted when no outcomes are configured.

Descriptions appear directly, with positions when rewards differ by rank.
Repeated descriptions are grouped with their positions, such as `1–3, 5`.
Large lists use **Load more outcomes** or **Load more positions**, and a failed
request offers **Try again**.
There is no separate Outcome tab or outcome-visibility setting. Older links to
an Outcome tab open Settings.

## Rewards and results

New outcomes describe rewards or actions that the organizer fulfills manually.
The app selects winners and records the configured awards; it does not perform
manual actions or allocate profile Rep/NIC ratings.

Existing Rep and NIC outcomes remain readable, including amounts and Rep
categories. Recorded awards remain available on winner cards. Leaderboard and
winner reward summaries are shown regardless of the retired visibility setting.
Use **Winners** (or the decision's custom Approve results label) for actual results.
