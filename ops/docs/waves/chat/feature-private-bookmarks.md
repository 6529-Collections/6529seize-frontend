# Private Bookmarks

## Save and remove a message

Open a message's More menu on desktop, or its long-press menu on mobile. Select
**Bookmark** beside the bookmark icon. The desktop action follows **Copy text**.
A saved message offers **Remove bookmark** with a filled bookmark icon.
The save confirmation includes **View bookmarks**.

Saving a message does not notify its author or publish it to your profile.
Bookmarks are private to the signed-in owner. Selecting a proxy keeps your own
bookmark collection; proxy permissions never expose the proxy profile's bookmarks
or grant access to messages your own profile cannot read.

## Find your bookmarks

Select **Bookmarks** in your account menu, below **Profile** and above
**My artwork documentation**. In the native app, use the account actions in the
menu drawer. The entry appears only when you have at least one bookmark you can
still access. Saving the first reveals it; removing the last hides it.

The private `/bookmarks` page lists saved messages across waves, newest saved
first. **Open in conversation** jumps to the original message. Use **Load more**
to browse older saves. Switching connected accounts loads that account's own
bookmarks. Signed-out visitors see a connect-profile prompt.

## Empty and unavailable messages

Removing the final bookmark keeps the page open with **No bookmarks yet**.
Deleted messages and messages you no longer have permission to read are excluded
from the list and its menu count. Bookmarks do not preserve a separate copy of
message content. Loading and request errors have separate states, with retry
available after a failed request. A failed save or removal restores the prior
bookmark state and reports the error.
