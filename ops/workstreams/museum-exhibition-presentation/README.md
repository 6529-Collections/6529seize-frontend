# Museum exhibition presentation

## Charter

Present `Keys and Gates` and `Conflict at Its Edges` as image-led Curated
Acquisitions within the 6529 Network Museum. The works and their curatorial
argument form the primary visitor experience. Scholarship, media authority,
rights, provenance, technical review, accessibility, preservation planning,
and public catalogue construction are the downstream accession work and remain
available as a subordinate institutional record.

The interface must preserve the exact lifecycle boundary:

- `Keys and Gates` is selected and unminted. No contract, token identity,
  title, custody, formal accession, accession number, or Collection membership
  may be inferred.
- `Conflict at Its Edges` was selected by the Museum Wave. Its downstream
  accession package is in progress; title transfer, custody receipt,
  certificate delivery, formal accession, and Collection membership remain
  open until independently recorded.

## Presentation contract

1. Works and natural-aspect media appear first.
2. The curatorial reading follows the image sequence.
3. The accession-processing record and sources are progressively disclosed
   after the public interpretation.
4. Work and artist routes use only exact source-record relations to reviewed
   media. Filename resemblance and status labels are not media authority.
5. Responsive Keys and Gates media comes from the source-safe public
   presentation manifest. Preservation locators never enter the visitor
   adapter.
6. Conflict at Its Edges uses the five exact display sources approved through
   downstream accession review. Each projection must match its Work and Media
   identities, historical Wave locator, Arweave display source, SHA-256 fixity,
   byte size, dimensions, display policy, and active accession amendment as one
   atomic gate. Credit and rights notices remain attached. The Wave selects the
   acquisition; it does not administer media-source review.
7. Images preserve their natural aspect ratio; the gallery does not crop works
   into uniform squares.
