## Help shape Stream

Stream is under community review before independent audit and deployment. You do not need to read every chapter. Start with the question that matters to you, then follow the details and source if useful.

- **Artists:** are attribution, approvals, payments, collaborators, and estate arrangements clear?
- **Collectors:** can you tell what you receive, what can change, and how refunds or failed reveals work?
- **Code reviewers:** do the exact contracts enforce the stated rules, including failures and replacements?

## Read the status beside each claim

**Built in this code** means an implementation exists, with any connection limits stated. **Agreed but unfinished** means an accepted requirement still needs implementation or evidence. **Still proposed** means the design awaits acceptance.

This review is pinned to `92ea123380917032f01aae09691141a2a72df935`. Older snapshots remain available as historical records. The older sale rehearsal uses a test-only legacy Core; please identify which path a finding concerns.

## Questions for reviewers

1. Can an artist see and approve every decision that needs their consent?
2. Can collectors understand supply, marketplace grouping, sale terms, and reveal status?
3. Do paid minting and refunds connect to the correct token and recipient?
4. Does finality close every intended change path while preserving usable artwork files?
5. Can emergencies and replacements preserve claims, balances, and history?
6. Which statement is unclear, missing evidence, or stronger than the code supports?

## Give useful feedback

Open the page's feedback panel. Describe what you expected, what the page or code actually says, and a concrete example. Select the relevant section or source lines where possible. The panel lets you preview the public message and applies the normal wallet and discussion eligibility rules.

The feedback records this review version and its source. A finding about a historical version should remain attached to that version. Please do not include credentials, private keys, personal information, or private operational details.
