## Summary

Mobile handoff links are excluded from the help corpus and blocked by its publishing validator. A dedicated Desktop post-installation answer begins with RPC activation, worker sync, and automatic TDH instead of app downloads or account pairing.

Add focused 6529 Desktop knowledge and detailed guides so help6529 can explain the dedicated Desktop menu, RPC activation, workers, automatic TDH, transaction repair/reset, NFT recovery, wallets, IPFS, and diagnostics. Add 6529 Mobile App Wallet knowledge for creation, import, connection, backup, recovery, deletion, and troubleshooting. The former name 6529 Core remains an understood alias, while answers use the official current names 6529 Desktop and 6529 Mobile.

## What Changed

- Publish detailed facts plus concise default/fallback answers, keeping local-data consequences beside destructive controls.
- Give generic app-availability questions a concise answer naming both 6529 Mobile and 6529 Desktop, supported platforms, and the official Apps download page. The companion backend recognizes incomplete discovery and download questions without requiring a platform.
- Separate definitions, setup, initial mismatch triage, and follow-ups after completed repairs. Add validated link metadata for a final More info footer.
- Match shorthand Desktop TDH/Merkle mismatch questions to the troubleshooting procedure.
- Reserve cross-platform clarification for Desktop-to-Mobile and browser wallet questions; ordinary “mobile Core wallets” wording belongs to Mobile guidance. Include natural “back up” aliases for Desktop wallets.
- Answer 6529 Mobile App Wallet questions, including the common legacy phrasing “Core wallets on mobile,” without sending users to Desktop-only controls.
- Distinguish local total TDH from website/profile TDH, and local recovery from on-chain holdings. Wallet exports are documented as plaintext secrets that must never be shared with the bot.
- Use exact native controls: Transactions `Reset to Block > Min Block`, NFTs `Full Refresh` versus `Reset All NFTs`, and TDH recalculation after repairs.
- Document source provenance pinned to Core commit `5d8a06e5ea66835f09ec89db40d4db714759462b`, with a maintenance guide for future native changes.

## Progressive reconciliation

Guide unresolved same-block TDH mismatches through 25%, 50%, 75%, then 100% of
indexed blocks, recalculating after each completed reconciliation. The bot asks
for the local Transactions checkpoint and computes specific starting blocks;
no date-dependent starting block is hardcoded. Corpus templates retain the range
for short follow-up replies. Their supported minimum remains source-maintained.
Publish these templates after the companion backend update so placeholders
cannot be treated as ordinary answer text by an older runtime.

After full-history reconciliation and recalculation still fail, offer transaction
Reset to Block proactively, with Min Block as the full-resync fallback. Explain
its deletion/reimport effects and carry the conversation through resync and TDH
recalculation; escalate persistent failures without repeatedly recommending resets.

Recovery advances only after completed work leaves a same-block mismatch. Failed
TDH calculation uses a dedicated error-diagnosis reply and retains its block range.
