PR Museum setup waits for package-manager locks before recovery after a timed-out dependency install, matching the existing deployed-E2E setup. - Visited chat retains its DOM and draft between tabs while hidden effects pause. Native view fades no longer remount the whole wave; the existing animation and reduced-motion behavior remain.

- The full native-competition pack runs in two independently reported shards within the existing job limit. Every case remains selected exactly once.
