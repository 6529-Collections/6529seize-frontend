## Summary

Repair browser-test setup and expectations exposed by the mobile startup and compact Network sheet changes. Application behavior and staging authentication remain unchanged.

## What Changed

- The fresh phone context inherits fixture storage state before the first navigation, preserving staging access without pre-hydrating the homepage. Its own read-only mutation guard protects that context.
- Search controls are awaited until visible and enabled instead of inspected immediately while navigation is loading.
- Tablet and touch Network coverage checks precise bottom alignment and stable height when widening the viewport, preserving the intended compact-sheet behavior rather than demanding a near-full-screen sheet.
- Main Stage app artwork coverage awaits API-backed section readiness and checks settings HTTP status before reading its payload. The leaderboard response is captured alongside the click, preserving tab ordering and artwork-to-response checks.
- Existing specs remain in their current packs; no exclusions or extra retries were added.
