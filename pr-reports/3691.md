# PR Report: Expand natural multi-wallet HelpBot corpus

PR: https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-frontend/pull/3691
Branch: `agent-prxt/helpbot-natural-multi-wallet` -> `main`
Related PRs: [Backend PR #1917](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-backend/pull/1917)

## Summary

The production HelpBot corpus now describes multi-wallet consolidation with
varied natural language for setup, effective limits, removal, and replacement.

## What Changed

- Expanded the consolidation corpus aliases across second, additional, other,
  and multiple-wallet phrasing.
- Kept the signal in explicit multi-wallet aliases instead of broad standalone
  action and quantity keywords that could collide with delegation questions.
- Excluded ambiguous capability wording about merely using multiple wallets.
- Clarified that these phrases refer to treating wallets together for supported
  6529 metrics.
- Added reusable consolidation-limit concepts such as maximum group size,
  wallet/address count, and capacity instead of relying on one question.
- Corrected the distinction between registration and counting: more than three
  addresses can have consolidation records, but only the last three count for
  consolidation purposes.
- Added natural removal/unlink terminology to revoke guidance and
  replacement/swap terminology to update guidance.
- Regenerated the public help-index artifact and protected representative
  language families with synchronization coverage.

## Frontend Impact

- Routes/views: Not affected.
- Components: Not affected.
- Generated API/client: Not affected.
- User-visible behavior: HelpBot retrieval can match a broader range of natural
  multi-wallet setup, effective-limit, removal, and replacement questions to
  the relevant consolidation guidance.

None.

HelpBot's corpus now covers natural variations across the multi-wallet
