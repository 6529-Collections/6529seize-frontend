# Sidebar interaction refinements

[PR #4668](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-frontend/pull/4668)

## Summary

The desktop bell previews notifications on hover and opens `/notifications` on click. The expand handle no longer stays visible because another sidebar control retains focus. Compact submenu icons indicate their flyouts with small centered chevrons.

## Frontend

- Notifications share the sidebar's 100 ms opening and 200 ms closing delays. Crossing between bell and popup keeps the preview open; mouse focus on an action does not pin it open. Arrow Down enters the preview and Tab reaches its actions. View all, close/Escape, outside dismissal, route/account cleanup, and mobile navigation remain available.
- Notification read semantics are deliberately preserved: loading the preview still automatically marks notifications read, and individual/explicit read actions retain their existing behavior.
- The expand handle appears on sidebar hover or keyboard focus on the handle itself. The touch fallback and hiding during collapsed-sidebar flyouts are preserved.
- Compact submenu chevrons are 14 px, vertically centered 4 px beside their icons, share their default/active/hover colour, and turn white with the item on hover. Existing icon positions, expanded indicators, and interaction targets remain intact.
- The preview uses a named native section, with document-level input tracking to distinguish keyboard focus from mouse focus.
- Navigation/notification documentation and Help6529 knowledge describe the new interactions.

## Risks / Follow-Ups

Full application browser packs were updated but not executed locally; the visual and interaction evidence comes from isolated components with mocked external boundaries. Check the real account/sidebar integration through the existing inbox and smoke packs.

The broader Jest typecheck reports diagnostic increases in four untouched config/encrypted-DM test files. Those files and the debt baseline are unchanged; details are recorded in the PR description.
