## Summary

Address two demonstrated production E2E failure causes: activating an identity information button before its handlers are attached, and selecting dates from pinned-drop link previews instead of the wave creation date.

## What Changed

- Keep the ID Statements information button disabled in server markup until hydration completes, then retain its existing interaction and dismissal behavior.
- Give the wave creation date a dedicated marker and preserve its visibility and ISO-date assertions.
- Add delayed-script browser coverage and strengthen server-rendered hydration coverage. Existing tooltip content, position and dismissal assertions remain intact.
- Update the identity statements guide and Help Bot knowledge for the initial disabled state.

## Risks / Follow-Ups

Saved failures also include a tooltip disappearing after opening. The reproduced hydration race does not establish the cause of every such disappearance; full-app browser validation of the deployed change is still needed before claiming this flow is reliable.
