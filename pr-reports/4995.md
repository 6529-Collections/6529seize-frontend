# PR Report: TDH day from the 6529 Network, and xTDH retired

PR: [Frontend PR #4995](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-frontend/pull/4995)
Branch: `claude/tdh-from-chain` → `claude/id-f1-freeze` (the identity freeze notice PR), then `main`
Related PRs: the identity freeze notice PR #4990 it stacks on, which merges first; the backend PR [6529seize-backend#2525](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-backend/pull/2525) "Read daily TDH from the 6529 chain behind two flags, and retire xTDH with identities".

## Summary

From the backend switch-on (planned Tue 24 Nov evening, once day 24 on the chain matches 6529.io's exactly), 6529.io reads each day's TDH from the 6529 Network.

- **TDH day.** The Network Nerd leaderboard shows which day that is ("25 Nov 2026, from the 6529 Network") whenever the API says the newest day came from the chain.
- **xTDH retired.** xTDH is retired when identities move to the 6529 Network; the network takes no xTDH and no grants over. On the freeze day grant controls are off and the xTDH pages say xTDH is being retired. From the identity switch-on, a day later, they say "xTDH was retired when identities moved to the 6529 Network." and the site shows no xTDH figures.

This PR's commits are the ones above the identity freeze notice PR's head, c869843751: the code, tests, docs and help changes, the e2e specs, and the report.

## What Changed

- **The TDH day stat** in `components/leaderboard/Leaderboard.tsx`, from `/api/blocks` `source` and `cycle_day` (`lib/tdh-chain/tdhDay.ts`).
  - Its hover text names the cutoff block and says validators agreed on the day's TDH on the 6529 Network.
  - For a day 6529.io computed itself the stat is hidden, so it follows a backend rollback.
- **xTDH retired** (`lib/xtdh/retirement.ts`, `hooks/useXtdhRetirement.ts`, `components/xtdh/XtdhRetiredNotice.tsx`), from `NEXT_PUBLIC_IDENTITY_FREEZE_AT`:
  - **The freeze day:** the notice on `/xtdh`, `/network/xtdh` and the profile's xTDH tab, and no create, Stop Grant or Revoke Grant. The figures stay.
  - **From the identity switch-on:**
    - `/xtdh` and the profile's xTDH tab show only the notice, and the tab leaves the tab bar.
    - `/network/xtdh` shows only its title and the notice, without the explainer.
    - xTDH leaves the stats row (the profile header, hover cards and its other callers), the network identities table (column, sort and mobile figure), the health page's "xTDH Granted" card and the drop participation card.
    - The Collected tab's Network view goes too: it listed tokens by the xTDH they receive.
    - Links with an xTDH sort or `collection=network` read as the defaults.
  - Levels show whatever the API sends. Before the freeze, or unset, nothing changes.
- **Copy and dates.** New strings in `i18n/messages/tdhChain.en-US.json` say 6529 Network. Dates are day first, as in the identity move notices.
- **API model.** `openapi.yaml` and `generated/models/ApiBlockItem.ts` gain `source`, `cycle_day` and `wallet_root`.
- **Docs and help.**
  - User docs for the Network Nerd leaderboard, the two xTDH pages, and the profile's xTDH tab and granted view.
  - One line each for the health dashboard, the network identities table, the profile header, the profile tabs and the Collected tab.
  - The help corpus records `network.tdh` and `network.xtdh`, synced to `public/help-index.json`.
- **E2E.** The readonly specs that run on staging and production read the retirement from `/xtdh` (`tests/support/xtdhRetirement.ts`). They check the xTDH tab, card and views while xTDH is live, and their absence or a reasoned skip once it is retired.

## Frontend Impact

- **Backend.** The stat needs the companion backend PR's `/api/blocks` fields; without them it stays hidden. The two deploy in either order.
- **Freeze time.** It is fixed at build time. Moving or clearing it needs a frontend build and deploy, so roll it back together with the backend's identity freeze.
- **Groups and waves.** Groups and waves on xTDH are converted to TDH at the identity switch-on, and the create flows stop offering xTDH, in the separate xTDH retirement PRs (backend #NNNN, frontend #NNNN). This PR doesn't change them.
