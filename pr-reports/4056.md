# PR Report: Video player rework

PR: [#4056](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-frontend/pull/4056)

Branch: `agent-prxt/video-player-rework` → `main`

## Summary

Make video seeking visible and usable, expose elapsed and total time, and keep videos uncropped while using the available artwork area on homepage and detail views.

## What Changed

- Move the timeline above the controls, with a subtle draggable circle and a larger interaction area. Retain the existing blue progress color and translucent unplayed track without a new track outline.
- Preserve the existing circular action buttons. A dark play/pause-and-time capsule keeps its position when paused; both the capsule and central play button resume playback.
- Keep controls visible during seeking and keyboard focus. Update time immediately while seeking, support longer durations and localized accessible values, and reset timing when the media source changes.
- Keep timeline state in a focused hook, cancel obsolete hide timers when the source empties, and preserve native seeking if pointer capture is unavailable for an ended pointer.
- Fit homepage, detail, and Wave submission video to the available width and at most 95% of usable screen height across desktop browsers, mobile browsers, and the mobile app. Preserve aspect ratio and center height-limited videos, accounting for shell headers, bottom navigation, and safe areas. Reserve the submission overlay header and artwork padding and respect the actual overlay height instead of retaining a separate fixed-height wave frame. Keep video size independent of loading listing, subscription, and ownership panels. Vertically center homepage artwork within its column, allowing its position to follow the column height without stretching the video. Preserve the full-column dark artwork background and vertical centering on The Memes detail. Center expanded wave submission video in the available area below its header, with details following the full artwork stage.
- Attach the timeline and controls to the contained video surface so they stay inside the artwork rather than extending across letterboxing, including fullscreen.
- Remove legacy height and width caps from The Memes detail video; it fills the available width until it reaches the screen-height cap, without cropping. Keep compact feed bounds and reserve a mobile frame for Next Drop media that requires explicit height.
- Reserve known NFT animation dimensions before video metadata loads, and discard measured dimensions when the source changes.
- Keep composer previews self-sized when their parent has no fixed height, and retain image layout when video metadata has no usable animation URL.
- Document the controls and update the Help Bot knowledge index.

## Frontend Impact

The shared player affects video in drops, wave submissions, and NFT artwork. Homepage changes cover both Next Drop and Latest Drop. Native-control templates, media fallbacks, and fullscreen paths remain supported. No generated API, backend, desktop, or environment configuration changes are required.
