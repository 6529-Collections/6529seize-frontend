# Signed records follow-up: fresh anchors, ordered batches, owners of profiles

[Frontend PR #4997](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-frontend/pull/4997) · [Backend PR #2526](https://github.com/6529-Collections/6529seize-backend/pull/2526) · stacked on #4994

## Summary

What the browser side of signed records' first callers need:
- `fetchRecordAnchor(now, { fresh: true })` asks again, and the cache never goes back to a lower height;
- `signRecords` takes `notBefore` (the earliest claimed time, inclusive), and claimed times rise through a batch, so a batch sent in several submits keeps its order;
- `record-owners.ts` names other people by their owner (the principal their primary wallet is bound to, or the wallet while bound to none), from `GET /signed-records/owners`. `ownerOfWallet` gives a wallet's own owner.

## Frontend impact

- **For people:** none yet. No page or hook imports the module.
- **API types:** `openapi.yaml` and the generated owners models match the backend; `6529 run generate` reproduces them.

## Deployment

After the backend follow-up. Until the backend wires in the chain gateway client, the owners and anchor endpoints answer 503 and the lookups throw, so nothing signs a guessed anchor or names a guessed owner.

## Tests

5 suites (39 tests) and `sign-write` (31) pass; `tsc`, ESLint and knip are clean.
