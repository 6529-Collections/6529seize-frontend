## Summary
The collector guide explains combining wallets, while its design page covers reciprocal links, grouping and the signing-order simulator. Both pages now display the cutoff and wallet limit supplied by the selected backend.

## What Changed
The guide formats activation time in UTC, explains the ten-minute signing delay and uses neutral guidance while settings are missing. The simulator explicitly models post-cutoff transactions and names this environment’s cutoff. Its illustrated grouping algorithm uses the backend maximum and ungated boundary; it waits for valid limits rather than substituting local constants. Help knowledge and the canonical guide describe staging timing independently of production.

## Dependencies / Rollout
The shared settings client and this guide now ship together in combined frontend #4219, after backend #2148’s settings contract is deployed. Production’s launch date belongs to backend configuration; no frontend date variable is introduced.
