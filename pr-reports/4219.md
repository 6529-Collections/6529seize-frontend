## Summary
Safe Collecting replaces the Delegation Center presentation with a guided wallet architecture and TAP explanation. The updated parent stack supplies environment-specific four-wallet activation timing and fixed backend wallet limits.

## What Changed
The entire public route family is under `/safe-collecting`: TAP Guide, FAQ and its articles, Consolidation Use Cases, Wallet Checker, Guided Setup, registration forms, primary-address actions and collection management. Former `/delegation` URLs permanently redirect with query parameters and section anchors preserved. All internal links, metadata, sitemap and help knowledge use canonical destinations. Search aliases let both `delegation` and `delegations` find the five main pages without changing their visible names.

The redundant NFT Delegation About page and navigation entry are removed; its former URL redirects to the hub. About retains `Safe Collecting & Wallets`, with Primary Address last. GDRC is the final Collections & Minting item. FAQ chrome is labelled `FAQ`; technical action and article titles retain delegation terminology. The unpublished documentation bundle is rebuilt with updated links and integrity hashes.

The TAP guide reads its displayed maximum from the existing backend settings response; it supplies neutral guidance while limits are unavailable. No local limit constant or new secret is introduced. Parent integration preserves the Safe Collecting rename and TAP advice while bringing in the shared settings contract, dynamic guide dates and Guided Setup safeguards. The help corpus and desktop troubleshooting docs explain Core’s separate wait for backend policy, preserved TDH results and full regrouping on policy changes, with updated source provenance.

Registration and primary-address pages provide `Back to Safe Collecting` during wallet restoration and while disconnected, including with a remembered signed-in profile. Shared form close buttons use the site's standard portal tooltip with hover and keyboard-focus support. A subtle note beneath the builder's wallet counter shows the selected environment's four-wallet signing opening time only before the activation cutoff and disappears at the cutoff in an open tab. Advance planning and signing eligibility are unchanged.

## Dependencies / Rollout
